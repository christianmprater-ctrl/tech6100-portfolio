  hero.jpg      - the big background image at the top (wide landscape, ~2400px wide)
  art-01.jpg    - gallery artwork (about 1600px on the long edge is plenty)
  art-02.jpg
  art-03.jpg
  art-04.jpg    - shown as a wide panorama tile
  art-05.jpg    - shown as a tall tile
  art-06.jpg
  art-07.jpg

Either name your files exactly like that, or open index.html in a text editor,
search for "const GALLERY" and change each "src" to match your filename.
(For the hero, search for "--hero-image".)
Use lowercase letters, no spaces (use hyphens). You can delete this note afterwards.
